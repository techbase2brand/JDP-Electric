# Privacy Policy

**JDP Electric Mobile App**
**Last updated: [DATE]**

This Privacy Policy explains how JDP Electric ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects your information when you use the JDP Electric mobile application (the "App") on iOS and Android.

By using the App you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information you provide
- **Account information:** name, email address, phone number, role (for example Labor or Lead Labor), and login credentials.
- **Profile information:** profile photo and other details you add on the Edit Profile screen.
- **Job and work information:** jobs you create or are assigned to, customer and contractor details linked to a job, work timer entries (start, pause, resume, stop times), timesheets and bluesheets, labor entries, material entries, additional charges, notes, photos and material slip images you upload.
- **Orders:** supplier and product selections, cart contents, order details and order history.
- **Support requests:** messages you send us for help.

### 1.2 Information collected automatically
- **Location data:** your device location, which is used to verify that you are at or near the job site and to run the work timer. See Section 4.
- **Device information:** device model, operating system version, app version, language, and unique device or app identifiers.
- **Push notification token:** an identifier used to deliver notifications to your device.
- **Diagnostic data:** crash reports and error logs, together with your user ID, name, email and the screen you were on when an error occurred.
- **Usage data:** how you use the App, such as the screens you open and the actions you take.

### 1.3 Permissions the App may request
| Permission | Why we ask |
|---|---|
| Location (while using the app, and in the background) | To verify job-site presence and keep the work timer accurate while the app is in the background |
| Camera | To take photos for jobs, bluesheets and material slips |
| Photo library | To select and upload images |
| Microphone | To record audio where a feature requires it |
| Notifications | To send job, timer and order alerts |

You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings. Some features will not work without them.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Create and manage your account and authenticate you.
- Provide the App's features: jobs, work timer, timesheets and bluesheets, material orders, invoices and reports.
- Track working hours and verify job-site presence.
- Send push notifications, including timer status and alerts, job updates and order updates.
- Show a running timer on your lock screen and Dynamic Island (iOS Live Activity) and in an ongoing notification (Android).
- Provide customer support.
- Monitor and fix crashes, improve performance, and develop new features.
- Keep the App secure and prevent fraud or misuse.
- Comply with legal obligations.

---

## 3. How We Share Information

We do **not** sell your personal information. We share it only in these cases:

- **Within your organization:** information you enter (such as time entries, bluesheets and orders) is visible to authorized users of your employer or business, such as administrators and managers.
- **Service providers** who help us run the App, under confidentiality obligations:
  - **Google Firebase** (Cloud Messaging for push notifications and Crashlytics for crash reporting)
  - **Supabase** (database and real-time notification services)
  - **Google Maps Platform** (maps, address lookup, geocoding and directions)
  - **Apple** and **Google** (push notification delivery, app distribution)
  - Our own backend hosting provider
- **Legal reasons:** when required by law, court order or government request, or to protect the rights, property or safety of JDP Electric, our users or others.
- **Business transfers:** in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets. We will notify you if your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.
- **With your consent:** in any other case where you have agreed.

---

## 4. Location Information

The App uses your location to confirm you are at the job site and to keep the work timer running accurately, including when the App is in the background.

- The work timer requires Location Services to be on. If location is turned off while the timer is running, the timer pauses and you will be notified.
- We do not use your location for advertising.
- You can turn location off at any time in your device settings, but the timer and job-site features will not work without it.

---

## 5. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App and meet our legal, accounting and business-record obligations (for example, payroll, timesheet and invoice records). When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:
- Access the personal information we hold about you.
- Correct inaccurate information (you can edit most profile details in the App).
- Request deletion of your information.
- Restrict or object to certain processing.
- Receive a copy of your data in a portable format.

**Deleting your account:** you can deactivate your account from within the App (Profile screen). You can also request deletion of your account and data by contacting us through the Support section of the App. Some records may be retained where we are legally required to keep them.

**Notifications:** you can turn off push notifications in your device settings.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us through the Support section of the App.

## 7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for working professionals and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.
